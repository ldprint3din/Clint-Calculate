Which material should I choose?

Not sure? Start with PLA. Pick another only if your part needs something specific.

PLA

Best for most parts
  • Rigid, sharp details, smooth finish
  • Good for prototypes, models, gifts, display pieces
  • Softens in a hot car or direct sun

PETG

Tougher and more durable
  • Handles heat and moisture better than PLA
  • Slight flex, so it resists cracking
  • Good for functional parts, brackets, enclosures

TPU

Flexible, rubber-like
  • Bends and absorbs impact
  • Good for grips, gaskets, phone cases, bumpers
  • Prints slowly, so it costs more per gram

What affects the price?

A 3D print isn’t priced by size alone. These are the main factors.

Material used

Heavier parts use more filament. Hollow shapes and low infill use less.

Infill

Infill is the internal fill of the part. 15–20% suits display parts; 40% or more makes parts stronger but heavier and slower.

Print quality

Thinner layers look smoother but take much longer. Draft quality is fast and fine for fit checks.

Supports and orientation

Overhangs need support material that is removed afterwards. How the part sits on the bed changes strength, finish and cost.

Questions people ask

Which file formats do you accept?

STL is easiest and works with the calculator. If you have STEP, OBJ or 3MF, or only a sketch or a broken part, contact us and we will help.

How accurate is the online price?

It is a rough range, usually close for simple parts. We always check your file and send a final price before we start printing.

How long does printing take?

The calculator doesn’t show exact times, but small parts are often ready within a day or two. Larger parts and batches take longer. We confirm the timeline with your quote.

What is the maximum part size?

It depends on our printers. If your part is large we can often split it into pieces and join them. Ask us before you design.

Is my design kept private?

Yes. The calculator reads your file only inside your own browser. Files you send us for a quote are used only for your order.

Can you design the part for me?

Yes, send us a sketch, measurements or a broken sample and we can quote the design work separately.
